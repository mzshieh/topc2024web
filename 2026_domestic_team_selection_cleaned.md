## Teams promoted from TOPC

The remaining teams will be selected based on the priority list submitted from the Taiwan Online Programming Contest (TOPC), with a maximum of ten teams per institution.

Priority Order for ICPC Taiwan Online Programming Contest Teams:

- Priority 1: The top team from each institution (and the top two teams from the host), ordered by their rankings in the ICPC Taiwan Online Programming Contest, capped at 30 teams in total.

- Priority 2: The top 40 teams from the National Collegiate Programming Contest (NCPC).

- Priority 3: Up to 10 teams recommended by CPE, admitted according to the ranking list provided by CPE.

- Priority 4: Teams that do not qualify under the aforementioned priority criteria, ordered by their rankings in the ICPC Taiwan Online Programming Contest.

Note: If any category of the priority admission list cannot be obtained 34 days prior to the ICPC Regional Contest, it will be temporarily suspended for that year. This limit does not apply if the competition is rescheduled due to force majeure events such as natural disasters.
